## Composition / Inheritance Decisions  

| Relationship                                | Decision                                           | Rationale                                                                                                                       |
|---------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Patient ----> ResidentialAddress (1)        | Composition: Patient owns its address              | An address doesn't mean anything by itself, not without a person living there. A patient can only have one residential address. |  
| Patient ----> ContactDetails (1)            | Composition: Patient owns its contact details      | Contact details mean nothing without it enabling communication to a person. A patient can only have one set of contact details. |
| Practitioner ----> ResidentialAddress (1)   | Composition: Practitioner owns its address         | Same rationale as above.                                                                                                        |
| Practitioner ----> ContactDetails (1)       | Composition: Practitioner owns its contact details | Same rationale as above.                                                                                                        |  
| Patient ---- Appointment (0..*)             | Association                                        | An appointment references patient_id, but it does not own it. A patient can have 0 or many appointments.                        |
| Practitioner ---- Appointment (0..*)        | Association                                        | Same rationale as above.                                                                                                        |  
| AppointmentManager ---- AppointmentStatus   | Dependency                                         | AppointmentManager imports and names it in its code. Dependency created through change_status() method.                         |  
| PractitionerManager ---- PractitionerStatus | Dependency                                         | Shared rationale as above.                                                                                                      |
