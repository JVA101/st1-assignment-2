
After entering the prompt into AI, it started generating a lot of feedback. 
It was not consistent in structuring its feedback, like suggesting recommendations in a few sections but not to all.
It had also included a comments on things that I had seemed to have done well on, inventing tasks past the prompt it was given. 
In many of the cases it wanted me to define the attributes of certain models in my requirements, like the appointment details and patient information.
This showed me that the AI was overextending the scope of a client brief towards a specification document that had not yet been given, extending the context of its feedback beyond the prompt. 
AI was able to give me some helpful feedback though, such as pointing out that I had not addressed the staff issue of having 'limited appointment history'.
I have also changed or edited some things in my requirements because of its feedback, such noting that the practitioner view of the appointments list has not been verified in the client brief or adding 'status' to FR-06 to address 'inconsistent appointment status information'. 
Requirements must have evidence to show that the work produced is within the scope of the issue, preventing the development of unnecessary features.
