## Problem summary
 A healthcare community clinic called SmartCare has asked assistance 
 from a junior developer to create a relatively simple software system to 
 help improve the management and workflow around their clinic. The major issue is 
 that in their current working environment, information is spread throughout 
 different mediums, such as spreadsheets, paper records and completing manual 
 processes. This creates a problem in trying to provide seamless service to their 
 patients and managing regular operations on a day to day. The clinic has 
 expressed having experienced issues with duplicated appointment bookings, 
 manual cancellation processes, a lack of reliable appointment history, and more. 
 Initially, they want a system that can support their tasks surrounding the 
 management of their appointments.  
 
---
## Initial stakeholders
1. **SmartCare** - Possibly need an update to their current work environment to 
improve and provide better services to their patients.
2. **Admin** - Possibly need a system that provides a comprehensive view of 
regular information that they used on a day to day in one place. 
3. **GPs** - Possibly need easier access to locating patient records and updates
regarding any changes to appointment schedules. 
4. **Patients** - Possibly need an improved booking system for their end, for ease of use
and viewing their appointment booking/s for any changes.  

---
## Initial features 
Appointment management  
- (Confirmed) Clinic has expressed difficulty in managing appointments so an automated bookings
should be able to flag and relieve issues surrounding duplicated bookings and the inconsistent 
appointment status information. 

Patient search  
- (Confirmed) Clinic has expressed having difficulty managing the location and access of patient
information. By placing this information in one database, the retrieve and use of patient information
will be easier for admin and GPs. 

Practitioner schedule view
- (Confirmed) Clinic has expressed having limited access to practitioner availability and having
inconsistent appointment status information, which would account for booking issues on both 
the clinic and patients end. 

Insurance processing
- (Provisional) The client has not explicitly expressed a need for this feature, but could 
aid in reducing the amount of manual processes that need to performed by the clinic and boost
work efficiency. 

---
## Questions for the client
Q1: Do they like the initial features listed above?  
Q2: What functions should the listed features be able to do?  
Q3: What types of data need to be included into the those features,
from patients, practitioners, and the clinic.   
Q4: Who should be able to have authorised access to the listed features above? Should
patients have limited access to appointment management or practitioner schedule view?  
Q5: What are their policies on storing and handling patient information?
