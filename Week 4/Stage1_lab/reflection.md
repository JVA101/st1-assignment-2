This week we looked into the differences between AI creating 
code and Software Engineers developing a program. AI has become 
a tool that is very convenient to use during the process of writing
code, but that does not necessarily mean that it is 100% reliable.
It can do what is asked of it given constraints and tasks, but 
it can still come up with hallucinations and make incorrect assumptions. 
It cannot comprehend a bigger scope that requires synthesis on multiple 
fronts, such as business, social impact, or performance. It operates on 
prompts provided on available context, which gives it a lack of 
responsibility, unlike engineers.
